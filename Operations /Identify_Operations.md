# Task 1: Identify Operations

| Op_ID | Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| OP01 | Perform Self-Check | Verify sensors and environmental-control devices. |
| OP02 | Record Artifact Information | Record the artifact's identification information. |
| OP03 | Load Environmental Profile | Load the artifact's required environmental limits. |
| OP04 | Monitor Environmental Conditions | Continuously monitor temperature and humidity. |
| OP05 | Check Door Status | Monitor whether the chamber door is open or closed. |
| OP06 | Correct Temperature | Restore temperature when it moves outside the permitted range. |
| OP07 | Correct Humidity | Restore humidity when it moves outside the permitted range. |
| OP08 | Verify Environmental Recovery | Confirm that the environment has returned to the permitted range. |
| OP09 | Activate Additional Environmental Controls | Provide additional environmental protection when required. |
| OP10 | Reduce Light Exposure | Reduce light exposure to protect the artifact. |
| OP11 | Generate Operator Alert | Notify the museum operator of abnormal conditions. |
| OP12 | Detect Significant Vibration | Detect vibration above the permitted threshold. |
| OP13 | Suspend Risk-Increasing Activities | Stop activities that could increase risk to the artifact. |
| OP14 | Verify Vibration Stabilization | Confirm vibration remains below the threshold for the required period. |
| OP15 | Suspend Conservation Activities | Stop normal conservation activities when the door is opened. |
| OP16 | Verify Conditions Before Resuming | Check environmental conditions and sensor status before resuming. |
| OP17 | Switch to Emergency Power | Switch to emergency power when normal power is lost. |
| OP18 | Record Power Incident | Record a power-loss incident when emergency power is unavailable. |
| OP19 | Perform Safe Shutdown | Safely shut down the system when power cannot be maintained. |
| OP20 | Verify Safe Artifact Removal | Confirm the chamber is safe before artifact removal. |
