# Museum Conservation Chamber

## Task 1: Identify Operations

| Op_ID | Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| OP01 | Perform Self-Check | Verify that all essential sensors and environmental-control devices are working correctly. |
| OP02 | Detect Artifact Placement | Detect when an artifact is placed inside the chamber. |
| OP03 | Record Artifact Information | Record the artifact's identification information. |
| OP04 | Load Environmental Profile | Load the required temperature and humidity limits for the artifact. |
| OP05 | Monitor Environmental Conditions | Continuously monitor temperature and humidity against the artifact's permitted limits. |
| OP06 | Check Door Status | Determine whether the chamber door is open or closed. |
| OP07 | Correct Temperature | Attempt to restore temperature when it moves outside the permitted range. |
| OP08 | Correct Humidity | Attempt to restore humidity when it moves outside the permitted range. |
| OP09 | Verify Environmental Recovery | Confirm through sensor readings that the environmental condition has returned to the permitted range. |
| OP10 | Monitor Recovery Period | Determine whether an abnormal environmental condition can be corrected within the allowed recovery period. |
| OP11 | Activate Additional Environmental Controls | Use additional controls to protect the artifact when normal correction is insufficient. |
| OP12 | Reduce Light Exposure | Reduce light exposure to minimize risk to the artifact. |
| OP13 | Generate Operator Alert | Notify the museum operator when an abnormal condition requires attention. |
| OP14 | Detect Significant Vibration | Detect vibration exceeding the permitted threshold. |
| OP15 | Suspend Risk-Increasing Activities | Temporarily stop activities that could increase risk to the artifact during significant vibration. |
| OP16 | Verify Vibration Stabilization | Confirm that vibration remains below the permitted threshold for the required stabilization period. |
| OP17 | Suspend Conservation Activities | Immediately stop normal conservation activities when the chamber door is opened. |
| OP18 | Verify Conditions Before Resuming | Check environmental conditions and sensor status before normal operation resumes after the door is closed. |
| OP19 | Switch to Emergency Power | Switch to an emergency power source when normal power is lost, if available. |
| OP20 | Record Power Incident | Record a power-loss incident when emergency power is unavailable. |
| OP21 | Perform Safe Shutdown | Safely shut down the chamber when power cannot be maintained. |
| OP22 | Verify Safe Artifact Removal | Confirm that the chamber is safe and no active protection response is underway before artifact removal. |
| OP23 | Authorize Artifact Removal | Allow the operator to remove the artifact only after safety conditions have been confirmed. |
| OP22 | Verify Safe Artifact Removal | Operator requests artifact removal | Chamber condition and protection status | Removal is confirmed safe or denied |
| OP23 | Authorize Artifact Removal | Chamber is confirmed safe and no protection response is active | Safety verification result | Operator is permitted to remove the artifact |
